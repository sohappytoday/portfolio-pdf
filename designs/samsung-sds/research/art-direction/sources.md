## Conflicts, limits and weighting

- Corporate and product surfaces are weighted highest because they consistently express enterprise scope, modular evidence and operational outcomes.
- Careers material is used only for role narrative; its people-oriented framing is not averaged into the master direction.
- The SCP portal is public, but no authenticated interface was accessed. No console UI, interaction pattern or implementation detail is inferred.
- Two user-provided browser captures were directly inspected: the public corporate home (S01) and the public Samsung SDS Cloud overview (S12). They provide two distinct rendered official contexts. Exact color sampling, font metrics, interaction behavior and image crops remain unobserved.
- Corporate/product captures establish a contrast pattern (luminous or dark focal field followed by calm evidence space); careers remains role-narrative evidence rather than a master visual source.

## Font and restricted-asset register

- Observed/restricted: SamsungOne (E10); license and PDF embedding permission for this portfolio use were not verified.
- Off-limits: Samsung/Samsung SDS/SCP logos and wordmarks, slogans, official imagery, screenshots, diagrams, icon packs, production CSS and page copy (E11).
- Safe alternative candidate: IBM Plex Sans KR under OFL-1.1, with Korean package documentation (E12). A future application pass must use licensed local static files and verify final PDF embedding.
