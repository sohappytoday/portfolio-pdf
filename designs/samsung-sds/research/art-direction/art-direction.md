# Samsung SDS — portfolio art direction

## Status

**Review-ready, not accepted for application.** Two public rendered contexts—the corporate home and Samsung SDS
Cloud overview—now supplement the current official corporate, product and careers evidence. The package still
translates abstract contrast, hierarchy and information patterns rather than asserting a copied Samsung SDS pixel style.

## Creative thesis

For a Samsung SDS-oriented portfolio, make enterprise engineering feel **auditable, modular and operational**:
a concise claim earns attention, then a reader can inspect the system, responsibility and outcome beneath it.
The expression should be a self-authored portfolio system, not a Samsung SDS interface imitation.

## Observation → principle → portfolio decision

1. **Proof before spectacle** — Corporate materials pair enterprise claims with figures and domain blocks (E02).
   Present each project claim with an explicit constraint, operating scope, metric or verified outcome.
2. **Sequence makes complexity legible** — Cloud and automation surfaces use numbered feature progression and
   labelled functions (E03, E05, E06). Use numbered diagnosis-to-operation sequences and self-authored diagrams.
3. **Taxonomy instead of console mimicry** — Public cloud materials classify capabilities by infrastructure and
   operations domains (E04, E09). Use layers, dependencies, ownership and status labels; never reproduce UI controls.
4. **Use human tone locally** — Careers material is more people-oriented than product evidence (E07, E08).
   Keep warmth for cover/about/closing while technical pages remain concise and evidence-led.

## Direction

- **Color and surface:** neutral, high-contrast base; one restrained self-authored cool-to-violet accent; a
  controlled focal field followed by calm evidence space. Exact Samsung SDS color values are not copied or sampled.
- **Typography:** use licensed local static **IBM Plex Sans KR** only after a future application pass verifies
  local files, Korean glyphs and PDF embedding. SamsungOne is research-only and off-limits (E10, E12).
- **Layout:** a short declaration followed by labelled evidence, numbered modules and clear vertical rhythm.
- **Imagery and diagrams:** use self-authored system maps, process flows and data fields. Official screenshots,
  architecture diagrams, photography and icons are forbidden (E11).
- **Tone:** factual, stable, specific about reliability, standardization, operation and improvement (E03, E06, E08).

## Page directions

- **Cover:** role statement + small capability taxonomy + operating promise; no branded hero image.
- **Architecture:** layers, dependencies, ownership and state; include a legend.
- **Result:** one verified outcome, then the mechanism and operating context.
- **Closing:** a restrained reliability/improvement signal with role relevance.

## Do / avoid

**Use:** concise labels, numbers where evidenced, self-authored operational diagrams, mechanism-to-outcome
explanations, visible constraints and responsibility.

**Avoid:** Samsung/Samsung SDS/SCP logos, wordmarks, slogans, product screenshots, official diagrams, icon packs,
production CSS, SamsungOne, invented metrics and simulated cloud consoles.
